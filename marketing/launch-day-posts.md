## Hacker News — Show HN

**Title:** Show HN: CLARIX – Ask questions across every meeting you've recorded

**Body:**

Most AI meeting tools summarise the call you're in. CLARIX indexes every transcript in a workspace and answers questions that span them — "what discount did we quote Acme?" — while the call is still running.

Answers are grounded in retrieved transcript chunks, and every one carries a citation that deep-links to the second it was said, so the claim is checkable rather than just plausible. That was the design constraint I cared about most: an unverifiable answer is worse than no answer when you're about to repeat it to a client.

Stack: Next.js, Postgres + pgvector for retrieval, AssemblyAI for transcription with speaker diarisation, Claude for the structured output, Tauri for the desktop app and its always-on-top overlay.

There's a fully processed meeting you can read without an account: https://www.clarixmeetings.com/demo

It has no users yet — this is the first time it's been shown publicly. I'd genuinely like to hear where the retrieval falls down.
