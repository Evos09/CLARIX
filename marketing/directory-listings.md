## Long description (BetaList, AlternativeTo, Futurepedia)

> Most AI note-takers produce the same generic summary for every meeting. CLARIX first
> detects the meeting type — standup, sales call, 1:1, planning session — and structures
> the notes around what actually matters for that type: blockers for standups, objections
> and next steps for sales calls, growth topics for 1:1s.
>
> Record directly in the browser or upload a recording. A meeting bot is optional —
> nothing joins your call unless you want it to. You get speaker-labeled transcripts,
> summaries, decisions and action items, and you can chat with any past meeting.
> Push recaps to Slack, notes to Notion, and action items straight into Linear.
>
> Free plan: 10 meetings a month, no card required.
