## Description (477/500 — REPLACE the draft's current text)
Every AI notetaker summarises the call you're in. CLARIX remembers all of them. Ask "what discount did we quote Acme?" or "what did we promise them last quarter?" and get an answer while you're still on the call — with a link that jumps to the exact second it was said, so you can check it rather than trust it. It records in the browser (no bot joins), and notes, decisions and action items come as standard. See a real processed meeting before signing up. Free: 10 meetings/month, no card.

## Maker's first comment (PIN this the moment you launch)

Hey Product Hunt 👋

Every AI notetaker summarises the meeting you're in. That problem is solved — Granola, Jamie and Fellow all do it well, and I'm not going to pretend I do it better.

The problem I kept having was different. A client asks *"what discount did you quote us back in March?"* — and the answer is somewhere in a transcript I'll never scroll through, so I say "let me check and come back to you." The meeting ends, the moment's gone, and I've lost a little credibility for no reason.

**CLARIX answers that mid-call.** It searches every meeting you've ever recorded and gives you the answer in about a second — with a link that jumps to the exact second it was said, so you can verify it instead of trusting it. That last part matters more than it sounds: an AI answer you can't check is a liability when you're about to repeat it to a client.

Here's a real processed meeting, no signup: https://www.clarixmeetings.com/demo?utm_source=producthunt&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=launch

Other things worth knowing:
• **No bot joins your call.** It records in the browser, or you upload audio.
• **Notes, decisions and action items** come as standard — table stakes, and included.
• **Free to try** — 10 meetings/month, no credit card.

I'm a solo maker and CLARIX has no users yet — you'd genuinely be the first. The feedback I want most: **open the demo and tell me where the summary is wrong.** More useful to me today than an upvote.
