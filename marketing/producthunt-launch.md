## Description (464/500 — replaces the draft's current text)
Most AI notetakers write the same summary for every meeting. CLARIX detects the meeting type — standup, sales call, 1:1 — and shapes the notes around what matters for it: blockers for standups, objections and next steps for sales calls. Record in the browser (a bot is optional) or upload audio. Recaps go to Slack, notes to Notion, action items to Linear. See a real processed meeting before you sign up — no account needed. Free plan: 10 meetings/month, no card.
