# CLARIX Branding Guidelines & Asset Guide

This branding kit was generated by analyzing the live visual styles, font declarations, and layout structures of your production site [clarixmeetings.com](https://www.clarixmeetings.com/).

---

## 🎨 Color Palette Specifications

The visual identity uses a bright, high-tech light theme offset by sharp, high-contrast violet tones:

*   **Primary Brand Color**: `Violet (#7c4fe8)` — Used for buttons, glowing vector connectors, highlights, and the logomark background.
*   **Secondary/Accent Color**: `Teal/Emerald (#10b981)` — Used for successful status badges, wave visualizers, and key highlights.
*   **Base Page Color**: `White/Light Grey (#fbfbfd)` — The clean, minimalist backdrop used across all layouts.
*   **Ink/Text Color**: `Slate/Dark Grey (#1f2937)` — High-contrast text lines and metadata.

---

## 🔠 Brand Typography

*   **Header Display Font**: `Inter` (Sleek sans-serif, bold weight)
*   **Code & Metadata Font**: `JetBrains Mono` (Crisp monospaced)
*   **Accents**: `Instrument Serif` (Elegant serif weight, italicized)
